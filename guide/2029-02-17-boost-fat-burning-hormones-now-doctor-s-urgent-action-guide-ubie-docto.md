What can I do to boost hormones that increase fat burning?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Regular exercise and a high-protein diet are two of the most effective ways to naturally boost fat-burning hormones. Strength training and high-intensity interval workouts stimulate growth hormone and testosterone, while protein-rich meals support glucagon and reduce insulin spikes, helping your body burn fat more efficiently.

However, hormone imbalances can also stem from underlying conditions like thyroid disorders, PCOS, or insulin resistance—issues no diet or workout alone can fix. Explanation

Boosting hormones that increase fat burning can be achieved through specific lifestyle choices. Exercise is a powerful way to stimulate hormonal changes that promote fat loss. When you exercise, your body releases hormones that help break down fat. This process is called thermogenesis, which means your body is using more energy and burning more calories.

Another way to help your body burn fat is by eating a high-protein diet. Protein can help your body feel full and satisfied, which might make you eat less. It also helps in building muscles, and muscles burn more calories than fat, even when you are resting.
