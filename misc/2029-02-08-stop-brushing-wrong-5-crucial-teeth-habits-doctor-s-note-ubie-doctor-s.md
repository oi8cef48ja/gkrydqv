Brushing your teeth is important to keep them healthy and clean. Here are some good habits to follow:

Brush Twice a Day: It is best to brush your teeth two times every day, once in the morning and once before bed. This helps remove food and plaque that can cause cavities.

Use Fluoride Toothpaste: Fluoride helps make your teeth strong. Make sure your toothpaste has fluoride in it.

Use Proper Technique: When brushing, use a gentle, circular motion. Don't brush too hard, as this can hurt your gums. Make sure to brush all parts of your teeth, including the front, back, and top.

Brush for Two Minutes: Each time you brush, do it for two minutes. You can use a timer or play a short song to help you know when two minutes is up.
