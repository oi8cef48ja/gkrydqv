# The Harley-Davidson x Todd Snyder Collection Is All About Old School Cool

You're about to see Harley-Davidson like never before. The renowned heritage motorcycle label teamed up with menswear label Todd Snyder (a favorite among Men's Health editors) to create an exclusive, limited-edition collection and it's good—really good.

The 119-year-old biker brand let Todd Snyder into its archives—a first for Harley-Davidson—to create an apparel collection that embodies the old-school motorcycle racing spirit, with new takes on the classic pieces that helped form shape motorcycle brand as we know it. Items in the collection include vintage-inspired sweatshirts, jackets for spring, graphic T-shirts, coveralls, and more.

This may seem like an unexpected look for the motorcycle brand known for what many refer to as "hogs" and the leather jackets to match. but the collection nonetheless carries the DNA of the brand. "Harley launched their first catalog in 1912 to sell parts, accessories, and apparel," Snyder shared in a press release. "And I was really inspired by the technical gear-racing sweaters that reminded me of vintage football jerseys—and the graphics, some which were over a century old and others from The Sixties and Seventies."

The capsule collection exudes a feeling of Americana, with embroidered embellishments and textured patches. No doubt, these will make a statement—but they're also the perfect addition to your everyday wardrobe essentials, whether you wear them to the gym, the office, or another well-frequented spot.
