What is Prurigo Nodularis?

Nodular prurigo is a skin condition characterized by itchy nodules, often associated with chronic scratching.

Typical Symptoms of Prurigo Nodularis

Itchy skin

Skin lumps

Skin over the entire body is red

Multiple areas of skin redness or red spots

Many lumps appearing on the skin

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Reddish-brown hard lumps or bumps on the skin

Hives or raised rashes

Diagnostic Questions for Prurigo Nodularis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have itchy skin?

Have you noticed any lumps on your skin?

Is your whole body red?

Do you have multiple red areas or spots on your skin?

Do you have multiple lumps on your body?

Treatment of Prurigo Nodularis

Treatment for prurigo nodularis focuses on relieving itching and reducing inflammation. Options include topical corticosteroids, antihistamines, and in some cases, systemic therapies like immunosuppressants or phototherapy.
