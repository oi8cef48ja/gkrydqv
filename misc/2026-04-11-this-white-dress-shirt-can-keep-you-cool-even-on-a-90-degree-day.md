What to Consider

Fabric & Material

Cotton

Cotton is the gold standard for dress shirts, with Egyptian and Supima cotton being the softest and most durable. Cotton blends with a bit of stretch add comfort and flexibility for all-day wear.

Poplin

Poplin (or broadcloth) is smooth and lightweight, giving a crisp, formal look.

Twill/Oxford

Twill and Oxford weaves are slightly heavier, more textured, and naturally resists wrinkles.

Fit & Cut

Slim Fit

Slim fit shirts offer a sharp, tailored look that’s cut closer to the body with higher armholes. It can feel snug if you have broad shoulders. Best for lean or trim guys.

Classic Fit

The most versatile cut, with more room through the chest and waist while still looking clean. Works for most body types and is a safe everyday option.

Athletic Fit

Trim at the waist while but roomier up top. Best for guys with broad shoulders, bigger chests, or muscular builds who hate feeling boxed in.

Collar Style

Spread collar

Spread collars are versatile and look sharp with a tie but also sleek open. The points angle out wider, leaving more room between them. Best for broader faces or shoulders since it balances proportions.

Point collar

The most classic style, with collar tips that point down closer together. Works on pretty much everyone, helps elongate the face, and is the most versatile for office-to-event dressing.

Button-down collar

The tips fasten with buttons to the shirt. More casual and relaxed, perfect for business-casual offices or weekends out. Also, works well under sweaters or open without a tie.

Wrinkle Resistance & Care

Non-iron shirts save time and stay crisp throughout the day, though they can feel stiffer. 100% cotton shirts are softer and more breathable but require ironing.

How to Style White Dress Shirts for Men

For formal events or business settings, pairing a white dress shirt with a dark suit is a classic choice that never fails—the crispness of the white dress shirt against the darker suit creates a nice contrast that exudes professionalism—and you should complement it with a tie and polished leather dress shoes.

Come less formal dress codes in business-casual settings or smart-casual environments, wear white dress shirts with chinos or similar styles to look sharp but also smart. You can also roll up the sleeves slightly or leave the top buttons undone to look more relaxed. Here, you can match these shirts with loafers or even dress sneakers to look modern while feeling as comfortable as ever.

To dress down with white dress shirts for casual outings or weekend gatherings, layer them under a well-fitted blazer or denim jacket to strike a balance between formal and relaxed. You can also wear these shirts untucked with jeans and khakis for a laid-back yet put-together appearance.
