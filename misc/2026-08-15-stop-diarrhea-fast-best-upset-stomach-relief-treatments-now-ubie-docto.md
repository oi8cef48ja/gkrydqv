Diarrhea and an upset stomach are common digestive issues that can usually be managed at home. While antibiotics may be prescribed for specific types of bacterial diarrhea, they are not a first-line treatment for most cases. The mainstay of treatment is proper oral rehydration—replenishing lost fluids and electrolytes to prevent dehydration. Over-the-counter rehydration solutions, clear broths, and small sips of water can help restore balance while your body recovers.

Because diarrhea and stomach upset can stem from many causes—viral infections, food sensitivities, medications, or underlying conditions—identifying the root cause is key to choosing the right next step.
