# The Warmest, Most Reliable Snow Boots for Winter

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

Cold feet are the worst right? Maybe we're being dramatic, but just a little bit of moisture in your shoes on a cold winters day can feel like you've got frostbite and you may have to amputate. Keeping your feet warm and dry this winter should be your number one priority. It doesn’t take that much snow (or wind) getting into your boots to ruin a trip into the office or a night out with friends. If you’ve been reaching for your summer shoes since winter begun, this list of the best snow boots is for you. Most boots and shoes lack water- and wind-proof qualities that are so essential in the winter months. While other types of footwear tend to look more stylish, they don’t provide nearly the level of protection of rugged snow boots. If you’ve ever experienced wet socks when you take a walk through the snow, you know what we mean. To help you combat this, in this list, we’ve focused our search on options that prioritize waterproof qualities.

Now that we’ve established how vital it is for your snow boots to be waterproof, let’s talk about the next most important quality—grip. The moment you step onto a sheet of ice or a snowdrift, you’ll realize how much this truly matters. In this list, we’ve provided boot options that are constructed on solid, grip-focused soles that will give you steady protection, no matter what conditions you face.

Another important factor is height. Winter boots are often classified as low, mid or high by their height. For snow boots, you’ll want to focus on options in the mid or high categories, so you can keep the snow outside where it belongs. This is important because no level of waterproof protection can extend your boots up your legs.
