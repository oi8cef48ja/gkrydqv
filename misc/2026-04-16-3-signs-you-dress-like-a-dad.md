# 3 Signs You Dress Like a Dad

Nowadays, you may spend more time and energy changing diapers than worrying about what to wear. But your kids shouldn’t be better dressed than you are.

[Signs You Dress](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/8572566173.html)

Here are three #DadStyle crimes you might be guilty of committing. Discover the simple fix for each mishap, and become a more fashionable father.

[Signs You](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/3935795940.html)

1. You mix patterns.

Pattern mixing is a dangerous business. Not even celebrity dad Chris Hemsworth can pull off a plaid shirt and camouflage shorts.

Keep your patterns within the same family—checkers, stripes, or dots—and then mix up the width or size of the patterns.

And stick with the same color scheme across your outfit. Style tip: It’s easiest to pair shades of gray or blue.

[You Dress Like Dad](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

2. You pair running shoes with jeans.

We totally understand the appeal of wearing comfortable shoes with jeans—especially when you’re chasing around kids.

It’s just that running shoes are made for running—and nothing else.

And while you’re at it, take a look at your jeans. You should reevaluate your denim supply every two or three years, paying close attention to fading or tearing at the hems and pockets.

3. Your fit is all wrong.

Loose, oversized shirts make you look heavier than you are—and nothing says Midwestern soccer dad quite so quickly. It’s always better to go with a slim fit, says Men’s Health Style Director Sandra Nygaard.

Wear a button-down that fits snug on your shoulders and chest and leaves you enough room for you to comfortably cross your arms.
