# Watch This Guy Eat and Train Like Arnold Schwarzenegger at His Peak

Bodybuilder and fitness YouTuber Jesse James West is back with a 24-hour diet and exercise challenge of another fitness great—and this time he's going for the icon of muscle icons: Arnold Schwarzenegger. The vlogger has already attempted Mark Wahlberg's notoriously rigorous diet and workout schedule, Chris Hemsworth's Thor diet and fitness routine, and Michael B. Jordan's Creed diet and workout schedule.

To emulate the schedule of peak bodybuilding career Arnold Schwarzenegger, West will be on another level. He starts his day at 5 a.m. with a meditation session just like Schwarzenegger, before heading to the kitchen to make Meal 1. He preps four eggs, 1/4 cup oats, honey, and two pieces of bacon.

"Not as filling as I thought," says West.

Next, he heads to the gym to do a high-volume back and chest superset workout inspired by Schwarzenegger's famous bodybuilding programming. He starts with 5 sets of 8 to 10 reps of bench press at 245 pounds in a superset with 5 sets of wide-grip pullups to failure.

"Not gonna lie, that was actually way harder than I thought it was going to be," says West. "I have a crazy chest pump and back pump."

The rest of the superset workout includes:

5 sets of 10 reps incline barbell press5 sets of 10 reps T-rows (replaced with bent-over dumbbell rows)5 sets of 10 reps seated cable rows5 sets of 10 reps dumbbell flys5 sets of 10 reps triceps dips5 sets of 10 reps reverse grip lat pulldown5 sets of 10 reps dumbbell pullover5 sets of 10 reps cable flys

"It's absolutely kicking my ass," says West before the last superset.

After the workout, West quickly has his post-workout Meal 2, which is just protein powder and water. But Meal 3 comes right afterwards. He cooks up 10 oz. of grilled fish, one cup of vegetables, a big salad, and two sweet potatoes.

West takes the opportunity to walk in Arnold's shoes, so he heads over to Venice Beach to visit the iconic Muscle Beach where Schwarzenegger famously worked out in the 1970s, as well as the legendary Gold's Gym where Schwarzenegger trained in his golden days (and is still known to drop in occasionally).

Back home, it's time for Meal 4: 12 oz. steak, 1 cup of broccoli and 2 cups of brown rice. He follows it up with Meal 5, another protein shake.

Next, he heads to a cryotherapy session for some recovery—a method that Arnold's son, Joseph Baena, is known to use. "That was the most intense three minutes of my life," says West.

He heads home to whip up Meal 6, which is actually just a container of cottage cheese and mixed nuts.

He tallies up his macros for the day, which come out to: 277g protein, 222g carbs, and 114g fat. "Arnold does a high protein, high fat, low carb diet," says West. "It's interesting, a lot of celebrities and people that are pushing themselves to higher levels deplete themselves of some carbs."
