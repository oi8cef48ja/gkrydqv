# Henry Cavill's Farewell to His Mustache Is Our Manliest Moment of the Week

Welcome to Manliest Moment, where we decide which dude crushed the subtle art of manliness the hardest this week. Next up: Henry Cavill bids farewell to a dear friend.

It takes a big man to laugh at himself. As you may have noticed, Henry Cavill is, quite literally, a very big man.

The Man of Steel actor recently appeared on Instagram in a comfortable-looking robe to dramatically mourn the passing of a close friend: The mustache he grew for Mission: Impossible - Fallout. "I know, it's hard to recognize me without KingStache," he says in the video. "Sometimes I have trouble even recognizing myself."

Because Henry Cavill is a living human being with two working eyeballs and a brain, he also took the opportunity to take a subtle dig at Justice League. "It's hard for me to admit, this is not CGI. He's really gone," the actor says.

You may remember that, due to some hilariously convoluted contractual obligations that had to be, on some level, a prank, Cavill was not allowed to shave his Mission: Impossible mustache while filming Justice League. An intern at Warner Bros. with only a basic working knowledge of Photoshop (we assume) was forced to digitally remove KingStache in post-production, which left the Man of Steel occasionally looking like the crazed bastard child of Heath Ledger's Joker and a just-ripe avocado.

But still, that was one hell of a 'stache. "I can tell you this, though. I will remember him. Alway," Cavill says, before throwing to an honest-to-Zod montage of his own mustachioed face set to Sarah McLachlan's "I Will Remember You."
