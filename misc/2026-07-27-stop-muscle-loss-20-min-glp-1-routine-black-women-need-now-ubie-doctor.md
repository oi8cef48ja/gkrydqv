The 20-Minute Strength Routine Overview

Equipment needed: A set of light-to-moderate dumbbells (5–20 lbs), a sturdy chair or bench, and an exercise mat.

Structure: 4 circuits, 2 rounds each

Time per circuit: 5 minutes (40 seconds work / 20 seconds rest × 5 exercises)

Total time: 20 minutes

Feel free to adjust weights or range of motion as needed. The goal is to challenge your muscles without compromising form.

Circuit 1: Lower Body Blast

Sit back onto a chair or bench, then stand tall.

Keep knees tracking over toes.

Step back into a lunge, drop your back knee toward the floor, then push through front heel.

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet hip-width. Lift hips into a straight line from knees to shoulders.

Feet wider than shoulder-width, toes turned slightly out. Lower hips, then press up through heels.

Stand on flat ground or a step, lift heels, hold briefly, lower down slowly.

Rest 1 minute between rounds.

Circuit 2: Upper Body Strength

Hands under shoulders, maintain a straight line from head to knees/toes.

Hinge at hips, back flat, pull dumbbells toward ribcage.

Press dumbbells from shoulder height to full extension overhead.

Hands on chair edge, lower hips, then press up.

Keep elbows close, curl weights with controlled tempo.

Circuit 3: Core & Stability

Align shoulders over elbows/wrists, keep body in a straight line.

Lie on back, arms overhead, knees bent at 90°. Lower opposite arm/leg, then switch.

Seated, lean back slightly, twist torso side to side holding a dumbbell or water bottle.

On hands/knees, extend opposite arm and leg, keeping hips level.

Support on forearm or hand, lift hips, hold the line.

Circuit 4: Power Burn & Cool-Down

Explode upward, land softly; or do a controlled mini squat and calf raise.

In plank position, drive knees toward chest, alternating quickly.

Lunge back, then reach arms overhead to engage shoulders and core.

Lift leg to the side, keep torso stable.

Managing Common GLP-1 Side Effects

Eat a light snack (e.g., banana, yogurt) 30 minutes before exercise.

Avoid sudden position changes.

Carry a small carbohydrate (like juice or a granola bar).

Monitor symptoms; stop if you feel faint.

Prioritize sleep (7–9 hours per night).
