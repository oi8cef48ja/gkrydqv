# Watch Chelsea Handler Perfect Her Squat Form in a New Training Clip

Chelsea Handler may be a famous funny woman, but she takes working out seriously. In a new Instagram clip posted by her trainer Ben Bruno, Handler schools viewers on how to nail squats. Although she has the form down, her exercise outlook might need a little improvement.

In the video, Handler holds a dumbbell to do weighted squats, with the comedian standing in front of a box meant to indicate the lowest point of her squat. Before beginning the workout, Bruno advises Handler to keep her chest up and make her squats “nice and controlled.” After a few squats, Bruno tells Handler to avoid tucking in her tailbone while at the bottom of her squat to maintain proper form.

After watching Handler for a bit more, Bruno notices her squat is too deep. He places a small bottle on the box behind Handler, effectively adding a few inches to her target low point which he says “cleans up the form.” It's another great lesson from Bruno: If you notice something messing up your form, don't be afraid to modify mid-workout. Perfecting form is more important than a super deep squat.
