# 5 Pieces Of Gear That Will Charge Up Your Workout

If you feel like you expect a lot out of your activewear, you’re not alone. We’re here to say that we are 100 percent on the same page. The reality is, the clothes we wear during our workouts should allow us to focus and push harder. They should help us level up, lift heavier, and crush PRs, not make it harder to meet goals. It also doesn’t hurt if that gear looks good too. There’s no denying that looking and feeling your best is an incredible confidence-booster—which might be just what’s needed to muscle through that last rep. Think of your gym apparel as a superhero suit of sorts. When you slip it on, you should be ready to tackle any challenge that comes your way.
