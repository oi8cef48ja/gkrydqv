2. Choosing the Right Form and Dosage

Ashwagandha comes in various forms. Here's how to pick and dose:

Forms

Standardized extract (capsules/tablets): Look for 300–600 mg per dose, standardized to 2.5–5% withanolides.

Powder: 1–2 teaspoons (3–6 g) mixed into smoothies or tea.

Tincture (liquid extract): Follow label instructions (commonly 1–2 mL, 2–3 times daily).

Dosage Guidelines

Starting dose: 300 mg once daily for 1–2 weeks, to assess tolerance.

Maintenance dose: 300 mg twice daily (morning and evening) or as directed.

5. Potential Side Effects & Who Should Be Cautious

Ashwagandha is well tolerated for most adults, but be aware:

Gastrointestinal upset: Nausea, diarrhea, or bloating.

Drowsiness: May amplify sedative medications or alcohol.

Blood pressure changes: Can lower blood pressure—monitor if you're on antihypertensives.

Thyroid impact: May alter thyroid hormone levels—check TSH, T3, T4 if you have thyroid disease.

Immune conditions: As an immune modulator, caution in autoimmune diseases (e.g., rheumatoid arthritis).

Avoid or use under strict supervision if you are:

Pregnant or breastfeeding

Taking sedatives, thyroid medication, immunosuppressants, or blood thinners

Diagnosed with a serious medical condition without doctor approval
