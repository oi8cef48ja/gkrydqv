Tips for Minimizing Side Effects

If you are planning to take nicotinamide riboside, a few simple strategies can help you reduce the likelihood of side effects and make the adjustment process smoother:

Start with a smaller dose Beginning with 250–500 mg daily allows your body to adjust gradually. This approach makes it easier to monitor how you feel and to identify whether NR is well tolerated before moving on to higher doses if needed.

Take NR with food Some people experience mild nausea or digestive discomfort when taking supplements on an empty stomach. Pairing your dose with a meal or snack helps reduce this risk and makes absorption easier on your digestive system.

Stay hydrated throughout the day Adequate hydration can help prevent mild side effects like headaches or fatigue, which occasionally occur in new users. Drinking enough water also supports overall metabolic health and can make the transition to NR supplementation more comfortable.

Give your body time to adjust Most mild side effects—such as bloating, flushing, or temporary fatigue—are short-lived. By staying consistent and patient, your system can adapt as NAD+ levels rise.

Work with your doctor Always check with a healthcare provider before beginning NR, especially if you are taking prescription medications or managing an existing health condition. Since NR influences cellular metabolism, your doctor can help determine whether it’s suitable for you and monitor your response over time.
