Explanation

Exercise is not just about losing weight; it has many other health benefits. Heart Health: Exercise helps your heart by making it stronger and improving blood flow. This can reduce the risk of heart disease. Muscle Strength: Doing exercises like lifting weights can make your muscles stronger and improve your overall body strength. Mood and Mental Health: Exercise can help you feel happier and less stressed. It can even help with conditions like depression. If you're experiencing symptoms that concern you, take Ubie's free AI symptom checker to better understand what might be going on with your health. Metabolic Health: Exercise can help people with metabolic syndrome, a condition that includes high blood pressure and high blood sugar, by improving how your body uses insulin and sugar. Remember, exercise is good for your body in many ways, not just for losing weight!
