When should you worry about liver-related pain on the right side?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Right-sided liver pain can be a warning sign, especially when it is severe, persistent, or paired with symptoms like fever, jaundice, nausea, or unexplained fatigue. Common causes include liver abscesses, hematomas, hepatitis, fatty liver disease, or gallbladder-related issues — some of which require prompt medical care.

Because liver pain can stem from many different conditions, identifying the likely cause early is key to getting the right treatment.
