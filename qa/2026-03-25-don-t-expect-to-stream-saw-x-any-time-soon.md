Halloween and Scream, for example, both rebooted their film series and had to grapple with the high expectations audiences undoubtedly put on them. Saw has an especially rough legacy to contend with. Over the course of its nine previous films, each film had varying degrees of quality in regards to both plot and its promised violent horror. While longtime fans are certainly familiar with the overall plot, Saw X hopes to add in more depth to what is one of the more exciting times in the series, while delivering scares worthy of an audience who ha seen nearly two decades of gory, thrilling traps and third act twists that catch them off guard.
