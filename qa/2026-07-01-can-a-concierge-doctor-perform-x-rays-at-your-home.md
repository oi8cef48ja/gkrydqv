What types of X-rays can be performed by a concierge doctor?

X-Rays are a noninvasive imaging procedure that can last less than 30 minutes. The results can be viewed in an hour and reviewed by your doctor. It is a form of electromagnetic radiation which is similar to visible light.

X-rays can be used to diagnose a wide range of conditions that are involved with different organs. The results collected from the image are interpreted by a specialist depending on the condition or illness that you have. Cardiologists can typically interpret chest x-rays and diagnose chest conditions like pericarditis. On the other hand, orthopedists can help detect broken bones or other fractures. Pomologists can look at X-rays to determine COPD and other similar conditions.

A concierge doctor can do everything that a traditional doctor can, including performing X-rays. There are many different types of X-rays that a doctor can perform, and the most popular ones include:

Chest X-rays

This type is recommended for people who are experiencing trouble breathing or have a persistent cough that won’t go away. The x-ray will take an image of your upper body, including the lungs, hearts, and ribs.

Abdominal X-rays

This type of X-ray includes images of your stomach, intestines, and spleen. An abdominal X-ray is needed if you are suffering from stomach pain and nausea as well as kidney stones.

Neck X-ray

This is recommended for people who feel pain or weakness in the neck area. This specific X-ray will take an image of your spine or vertebrae to determine if there is a fracture, dislocated joint, or infection.

Hand X-ray

A hand x-ray will be performed if you are experiencing pain or have a hand injury. This type of X-ray can determine if there are any joint abnormalities or broken bones.
