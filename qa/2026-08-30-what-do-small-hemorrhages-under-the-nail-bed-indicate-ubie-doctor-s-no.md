Explanation

Splinter hemorrhages are tiny blood spots under the nails that look like splinters. They can happen if you injure your nail, but sometimes they mean something more serious.

Possible Causes

Injury: Often, these appear after hitting or injuring your nail.

Health Conditions: They can be linked to medical issues like psoriasis, which affects the skin and nails, or even heart problems.
