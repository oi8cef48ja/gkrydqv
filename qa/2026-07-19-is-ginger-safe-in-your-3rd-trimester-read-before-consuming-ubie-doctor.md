Internal Medicine

Ginger is generally considered safe to consume during the third trimester of pregnancy and may help relieve nausea, indigestion, and mild pregnancy-related discomfort. Most experts recommend limiting intake to about 1,000 mg per day. Because responses vary and every pregnancy is unique, it's always best to consult your healthcare provider before adding ginger supplements or teas to your routine.
