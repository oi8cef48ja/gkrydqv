Shoulder pain combined with difficulty raising your arm may indicate several conditions, including diabetes-related frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), rotator cuff injuries, or nerve damage following surgery. These symptoms should not be ignored, as early diagnosis often leads to better outcomes and can prevent long-term mobility loss.

Shoulder pain and difficulty in raising your arm can be concerning, and there are several potential causes. One possible cause is related to diabetes. People with diabetes can experience shoulder issues, like a frozen shoulder, which may limit shoulder movement and cause pain. Another potential cause is nerve injuries that can occur after surgeries or traumas, involving the neck or spine. Nerve problems can lead to weakness or inability to move the arm properly. Additionally, radiation therapy for cancer performed close to the shoulder, can sometimes lead to shoulder and arm problems, affecting movement and causing discomfort.

If you are experiencing these symptoms, it is important to talk to a healthcare provider.
