Explanation

Sore nipples can happen for several reasons other than pregnancy. One possible cause is Raynaud's phenomenon of the nipple, which involves a change in blood flow and can be treated. This condition may cause pain or discomfort in the nipple area.

Another reason for sore nipples could be related to nipple discharge. While the reference provided discusses bloody nipple discharge, any kind of discharge might be associated with soreness. It's important to monitor for any unusual discharge, as it might be a sign of a condition that needs medical attention.
