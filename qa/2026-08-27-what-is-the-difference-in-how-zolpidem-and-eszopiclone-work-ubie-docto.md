Zolpidem and eszopiclone are prescription sleep medications that both target GABA receptors in the brain, but they work in slightly different ways. Zolpidem (brand name Ambien) primarily helps you fall asleep faster and is typically shorter-acting. Eszopiclone (brand name Lunesta) has a longer half-life, which means it can help you both fall asleep and stay asleep through the night.
