Vitamin B1 is also called thiamin. It helps the body utilize the carbohydrates it intakes by converting them into an energy source. Low B1 means low energy and lower brain and nervous system functionality.

Vitamin B2 is also called riboflavin. This is another essential B vitamin that helps the body convert its carbohydrates from food into fuel for the body; this is also known as glucose. Low levels of riboflavin may cause fatigue, blurred vision, swollen throat, skin issues, and even depression.

B3 is also called Niacin. This vitamin helps convert food to energy and acts as a coenzyme for energy transfers within cells. Niacin helps keep the hair, skin, and nervous system healthy. Low vitamin B3 can cause rough skin areas or rashes, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, fatigue, and even aggressive or paranoid behavior. Having low b3, you may have other symptoms such as indigestion or fatigue.

B12 plays an important role in energy production in the body. This is because it helps transform food energy into energy that the body’s cells can actually use. Having a B12 deficiency may cause weakness, extreme fatigue, lightheadedness, pale skin, shortness of breath, and heart palpitations.

Vitamin D is a common deficiency people can have, and low energy is the most common symptom. Vitamin D helps the cells generate energy and use oxygen to power parts of the body.

Vitamin D deficiency may cause bones to become brittle. It also causes extremely low energy. It may also cause the immune system to be weak.

Magnesium helps reduce fatigue or exhaustion. This mineral is important in helping the nervous system continue working properly. This mineral also helps the body relax so that it can relax and rest long enough to replenish energy levels. It then will cause the body to have more energy in the long run. Being deficient in magnesium can cause muscle cramps, hormone imbalances, anxiety and depression, high blood pressure, extremely low energy, and poor bone health.

Zinc has been known to reduce fatigue significantly. It is also shown to help improve the quality of sleep if it is taken directly before bed. This, in turn, helps you have more energy when you are awake. Symptoms of low zinc can be loss of appetite, hair loss, and low immune function.

Creatine helps maintain a supply of energy sustainable to muscles. It is also found in other areas of the body, such as the brain and heart. It helps produce energy when using muscles for day-to-day activity or even high-intensity exercises. Having low creatine can create liver disease, lack of strength and energy, and feelings of being dizzy.

Iron is critical for helping produce energy in the body. It is a part of red blood cells that combines with oxygen. This oxygen is then carried throughout the entire body, which is then transformed into energy for the cells to use during different functions. Being deficient in iron can cause extreme fatigue, chest pain, headaches, inflammations, and weakness, among other issues.

Coenzyme Q10 is a powerful antioxidant that helps with energy levels. This is because it stimulates the mitochondria of the cells, which produce more energy or ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate). This decreases while we age, making it more important to supplement. Health conditions such as heart disease, brain disorders, diabetes, or cancer can be linked to low levels of this.
