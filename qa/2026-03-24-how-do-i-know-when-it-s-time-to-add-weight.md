Victor, Lansing, MI asked:

How do I know when it's time to add weight?

Answer:

Make sure you can perform all your repetitions with your current weight for two consecutive workouts. Example: When you reach the upper limit of your repetition range with, say, 200 pounds--12 reps, maybe, if you're lifting to pack on size--make sure you perform 12 reps with 200 pounds during your next workout before adding more. And always add weight gradually: about 5 percent for each new step.
