Is it common to experience leaking urine after having a baby, and what can be done about it?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

It is common for women to experience urine leakage after having a baby. Exercises and other treatments can help manage this issue.

Explanation

After having a baby, many women experience leaking urine, which is also known as urinary incontinence. This is quite common and can happen because the muscles that help control the bladder may get weaker during pregnancy and childbirth.
