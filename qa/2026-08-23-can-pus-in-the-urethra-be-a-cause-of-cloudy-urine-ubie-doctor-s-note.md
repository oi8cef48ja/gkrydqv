Cloudy urine can be caused by several factors, and one of them is the presence of pus. Pus is a thick fluid that results from an infection, often containing white blood cells, bacteria, and dead tissue. When there is an infection in the urinary tract, pus mixed in with urine can make it appear cloudy.

Causes of Cloudy Urine:

Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): These are common infections that can cause pus in the urine. UTIs can occur in any part of the urinary system, including the urethra, bladder, or kidneys.

Inflammation: Conditions like urethritis, which is inflammation of the urethra, can also lead to pus formation.

Symptoms to Watch For:

Pain or burning sensation during urination

Frequent urge to urinate

Foul-smelling urine

If you notice cloudy urine along with other symptoms, it might be a sign of an infection, and it's important to seek medical advice for proper diagnosis and treatment. Always remember to drink plenty of water to help flush out your urinary system.
