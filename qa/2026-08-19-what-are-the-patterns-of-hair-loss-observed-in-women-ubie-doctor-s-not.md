Female pattern hair loss is a common condition where women experience thinning hair. It usually begins with a widening of the parting line on the scalp. Over time, this can lead to a more diffuse thinning across the top of the head.

Key Points:

Thinning Hair: The hair becomes thinner, especially at the crown and the top of the head. This is the most noticeable sign.

Overall Hair Loss: Unlike men, women usually do not experience complete baldness. Instead, there is a general thinning of hair.
