Explanation

Nerve pain in the groin and inner thigh can be caused by issues with the genitofemoral nerve or obturator nerve.

Genitofemoral Neuralgia: This condition involves pain from the genitofemoral nerve. Treatment options include medications to manage pain and physical therapy to improve movement and reduce discomfort.

Obturator Nerve Entrapment: This condition can cause groin pain, especially in athletes. Treatment can involve rest, physical therapy, and sometimes surgery if the nerve is severely trapped.

Each treatment should be considered based on individual needs and medical advice. Always consult with a healthcare professional for a treatment plan tailored to your specific condition.
