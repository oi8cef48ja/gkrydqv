Lyme disease is sometimes misdiagnosed as depression because the two conditions share overlapping symptoms, including persistent fatigue, low mood, brain fog, sleep disturbances, and difficulty concentrating. Because Lyme disease is caused by a tick-borne bacterial infection, missing the correct diagnosis can delay treatment and worsen symptoms over time. If you're experiencing symptoms that feel like depression but aren't improving, or if you've had possible tick exposure, it's worth exploring other causes. Lyme disease and depression can have overlapping symptoms, which might lead to misdiagnosis. According to the medical literature, Lyme disease can cause symptoms such as tiredness, mood changes, and cognitive difficulties, which can also be seen in depression.

Similar Symptoms: Both conditions can cause fatigue, difficulty concentrating, and mood changes, making them hard to tell apart.
