When your eyes feel heavy and tired all the time, it could be due to a few different reasons.

Lack of Sleep: Not sleeping enough can make your eyes feel tired. When you don't get enough rest, your eyes may feel heavy and uncomfortable.

Medication Side Effect: Various medications can cause fatigue as a side effect, which can make your eyes feel tired and heavy. For example, some antihistamines, some pain medications, and some antidepressants can make you feel tired.

Some Health Conditions: Some health conditions can cause eye symptoms. For example, dry eye disease is when your eyes don't make enough tears so they can get dry and feel tired, heavy, and uncomfortable. Another common example is sleep apnea. A less common example is fibromyalgia, which is a condition that can cause pain all over the body and also make your eyes feel tired and heavy.
