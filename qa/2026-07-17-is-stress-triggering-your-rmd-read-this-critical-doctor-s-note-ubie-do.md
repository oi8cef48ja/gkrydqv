Sleep-Related Rhythmic Movement Disorder (RMD) is a sleep condition marked by repetitive, rhythmic motions—such as head banging, body rocking, or leg rolling—that typically occur as a person transitions into sleep. While RMD is most common in infants and young children, it can persist or appear in adults, and emerging observations suggest stress, anxiety, and emotional overload may play a role in triggering or worsening symptoms in some individuals. However, current medical references do not definitively establish a direct link between RMD and emotional stress, and more research is needed to clarify the relationship.

If you or a loved one are experiencing unexplained rhythmic movements during sleep, disrupted rest, or heightened stress alongside these symptoms, understanding what's happening is the first step toward relief.
